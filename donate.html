Give with Zelle, straight from your bank app.

Zelle charges no processing fees, so your full gift goes to our programs: youth craft workshops, food security, healthcare support, and disaster relief.

Our Zelle tag
hastahopefoundation

Before you send, check that the recipient shows as Hasta Hope Foundation.

How to donate

  1. Open Zelle

    Open your bank's app, go to Zelle, and choose Send.

  2. Enter our tag

    Type hastahopefoundation as the recipient.

  3. Confirm the name

    Make sure it shows Hasta Hope Foundation. Zelle payments can't be reversed.

  4. Add a memo

    Enter your amount, and add your name and email in the memo so we can thank you. Write "Crochet For Hope" to support that initiative.

Need a receipt?

Email crochetforhope4u@gmail.com with the date and amount of your gift, and we'll send one.

Can't find Zelle?

Zelle is built into most US banking apps. If yours doesn't have it, email us and we'll help you give another way.
